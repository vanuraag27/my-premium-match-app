# VibeKey Child Safety & Protection Policy

**Effective Date:** August 2026

## Zero-Tolerance Policy
VibeKey maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), and any form of child harm.

## Age Restrictions
VibeKey is exclusively intended for adults aged 18 and older. We enforce age verification during user onboarding to prevent minors from creating accounts.

## In-App Reporting Mechanism
Users can immediately report suspicious behavior, illegal content, or safety concerns directly within the app by navigating to any user profile or chat thread and selecting **Report**.

## Moderation & Law Enforcement Cooperation
All reports are reviewed immediately. Any account involved in CSAE or CSAM violations will be permanently terminated. We preserve relevant data and actively report incidents to regional and national authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
